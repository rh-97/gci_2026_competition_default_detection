About In-Class Competition
The in-class competition is modified version of the 
“Home Credit Default Risk” 
competition originally held on Kaggle.
Your task is to 
build a model to predict the probability of customer loan default based on the provided customer data.
Source: Kaggle, “Home Credit Default Risk” — Image: Home Credit Group
https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/home-credit-default-risk/overview/description
